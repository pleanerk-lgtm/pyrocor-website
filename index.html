What We Trade01

How We Trade02

Principal trading.
Not brokerage.

Pyrocor Trading operates as a principal. We take positions, commit capital and stand behind our trades. This is not an intermediary or agency model.

We source supply, identify buyers, structure the commercial and logistics terms and execute. Our counterparty relationships span producers, smelters, processors, industrial consumers and other trading houses across Southern Africa, Europe, Asia and the Americas.

Engagements range from single-transaction spot trades through to structured term supply arrangements, depending on the commodity and counterparty requirements.

01
Principal Positions
We commit as principal and stand behind our trades. No agency, no brokerage — direct accountability on every deal.
02

We trade physical product. Our expertise is in the procurement, logistics and sale of real commodity volumes across real markets.
03
Southern Africa and Global
Primary market is Southern Africa. Our counterparty network reaches across 20+ countries internationally.
04
Flexible Deal Structures
Spot trades, term supply or structured offtake. We adapt the commercial structure to suit the opportunity.

Our ESG Framework01

ESG at the heart
of every decision.

Pyrocor operates across commodity sectors that sit at the heart of the global sustainability agenda: minerals critical to the energy transition, cleaner energy commodities, agricultural proteins supporting food security, base metals essential to modern industry and industrial chemicals underpinning more efficient production.

Our principal has been active at this intersection for over two decades, navigating markets where environmental standards, social responsibility and governance quality are increasingly core to commercial viability.

We apply ESG principles across our trading activity, advisory engagements and investment decisions — including how we select counterparties, structure transactions and which opportunities we choose to support.

Environmental
We prioritise commodity sectors and transactions that support the energy transition, reduce emissions intensity and enable more efficient use of natural resources. Environmental responsibility is core to our market thesis.
Social
We support fair dealing with all counterparties, uphold labour and human rights standards across our supply chains and contribute to the development of sustainable commodity markets in Southern Africa.
Governance
Pyrocor operates with full transparency, clear contractual frameworks and a zero-tolerance approach to corruption and unethical practice. We hold every counterparty and investee to the same standards.
