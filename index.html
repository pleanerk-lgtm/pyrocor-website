About Pyrocor

Pyrocor is a specialist commodity business headquartered in Johannesburg, operating across trading, advisory and capital investment.

Our Story01

The firm behind
the expertise.

Pyrocor is a specialist commodity business led by a principal with over two decades of hands-on trading experience across Southern Africa and global commodity markets.

The business was built around a straightforward insight: the best opportunities in commodity markets sit in the gaps. Between what is technically possible and what the market has priced. Between what a large firm is too slow to pursue and what a smaller operator lacks the knowledge to structure.

Twenty years of understanding commodity science at a process level means we can work with complex minerals and non-standard products that most traders will not touch. That technical depth is the moat. It is not easily acquired and it does not come from reading a market report.

We work with commodity trading businesses, industrial producers, consumers and project developers that need a knowledgeable, well-connected partner operating with full accountability.

Pyrocor's counterparty network spans more than 20 countries. Our commodity track record covers five sectors: industrial chemicals, minerals, proteins, power and energy and base metals.

Our Trading Principles03

The science of the trade
is the advantage.

Most commodity traders work from price screens and market reports. We work from first principles. Understanding what a material is, how it was produced, what its downstream options are and what a smelter or refinery will actually pay for it is what allows us to find and structure trades that others cannot.

We operate as principal. We commit our own capital, carry the risk and stand behind every deal. There is no intermediary layer and no ambiguity about who is responsible for delivery.

And we move fast. A small principal-run business with no approval chains can assess an opportunity and commit the same day. In complex material trades where counterparties need certainty quickly that responsiveness is a real advantage.

01
Technical Knowledge
We understand metallurgical processes, assay specifications and refinery requirements at a working level. That is what allows us to assess and price complex materials that most traders will not engage with.
02
Complexity as Opportunity
Polymetallic concentrates, intermediates and off-specification products sit in a part of the market where most participants lack the technical background to operate. That is where the pricing gaps are and that is where we focus.
03
Speed of Commitment
No committees and no approval chains. We assess an opportunity and commit. In complex material trades where counterparties need a fast and reliable answer that speed matters.
04
Principal Accountability
We commit our own capital and stand behind every trade. No agency model, no intermediary layers and complete clarity about who is responsible from deal to delivery.

Our ESG Framework01

ESG at the heart
of every decision.

Pyrocor operates across commodity sectors that sit at the heart of the global sustainability agenda: minerals critical to the energy transition, cleaner energy commodities, agricultural proteins supporting food security, base metals essential to modern industry and industrial chemicals underpinning more efficient production.

Our principal has been active at this intersection for over two decades, navigating markets where environmental standards, social responsibility and governance quality are increasingly core to commercial viability.

We apply ESG principles across our trading activity, advisory engagements and investment decisions including how we select counterparties, structure transactions and which opportunities we choose to support.

Environmental
We prioritise commodity sectors and transactions that support the energy transition, reduce emissions intensity and enable more efficient use of natural resources. Environmental responsibility is core to our market thesis.
Social
We support fair dealing with all counterparties, uphold labour and human rights standards across our supply chains and contribute to the development of sustainable commodity markets in Southern Africa.
Governance
Pyrocor operates with full transparency, clear contractual frameworks and a zero-tolerance approach to corruption and unethical practice. We hold every counterparty and investee to the same standards.
Our Commitments02

What we commit
to in practice.

Our ESG commitments are practical and verifiable. They reflect the way we have always operated and the standards we hold across trading, advisory and investment.

Energy Transition Alignment
We actively develop market access and investment exposure to commodities critical to cleaner energy, battery technology and decarbonisation infrastructure.
Responsible Counterparty Selection
We conduct due diligence on the environmental and governance standards of our trading partners and investment counterparties. We will not work with entities that fall short of our standards.
Anti-Corruption & Full Compliance
Zero tolerance for bribery, corruption or sanctions violations. We operate within full legal and regulatory compliance in all jurisdictions.
Food Security & Agricultural Responsibility
In our protein trading activities we uphold responsible sourcing standards and support supply chains that contribute to food security across Southern Africa.
Supply Chain Traceability in Complex Metals
Trading complex base metal intermediates and polymetallic concentrates requires rigorous counterparty due diligence and provenance verification. We maintain clear chain-of-custody documentation on all complex metal trades and will not engage with material of unknown or unverifiable origin.
Transparent Reporting
We commit to open and honest communication with all stakeholders on ESG matters and support clients in improving their own ESG reporting.
