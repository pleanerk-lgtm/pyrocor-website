01

Energy and complex minerals

Energy requirements need alignment between product quality, delivery timing, pricing and payment. Complex mineral streams also require attention to composition, product form, recoverable value and the requirements of the intended recipient.

These considerations sit at the centre of our trading focus. The wider catalogue covers manganese and chrome ores, specialist mineral materials, agricultural commodities and cementitious materials.

02

Start with the material

A useful trading brief identifies the product form, specification, quantity, quality information and intended delivery basis. For minerals, this may include assay data, moisture, relevant impurities and sampling arrangements. For other commodities, grade, packaging, certification and handling requirements may be equally important.

Clear information at the outset helps establish whether the proposed material and the commercial requirement are aligned.

03

Commercial structure

Pricing, payment and delivery should work together. A fixed price, a benchmark-linked price and a formula-based settlement create different exposures. Quotation periods, currency, quality adjustments and inspection arrangements need to reflect the transaction rather than rely on a generic template.

The same applies to title, risk transfer, documentation and claims procedures. Transport and storage requirements can form part of the commercial planning; the responsibilities of operators and counterparties must be defined separately.

04

More than one route to a transaction

A requirement may concern a single parcel, a recurring programme, a purchase mandate or a proposed offtake. The appropriate approach depends on the product, the parties and the commercial conditions.

Where price risk, foreign exchange or funding affects the transaction, Advisory and Capital can be considered alongside the trading requirement.

05

Prepare a trading enquiry

Provide the material, available specification, indicative quantity, delivery location or basis, timing and whether the enquiry concerns buying, selling or offtake. Detailed assays, contracts and confidential commercial information can follow once the scope is established.
