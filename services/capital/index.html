01

Structured trade finance

A trade-finance requirement is defined by the movement of product, documents and cash. The structure must account for when the commodity is paid for, when it is delivered, how it is inspected and when payment is collected.

Potential structures can involve prepayment, documentary credit, receivables or inventory-related funding. Suitability depends on the transaction, the parties, available security and the requirements of the relevant funding counterparties.

The work begins with a coherent trade cycle—not an assumption that a particular instrument or credit approval will be available.

02

Commodity funding

A commodity position may require capital between acquisition and sale, during storage, or across a recurring supply programme. Quality evidence, title, insurance, custody, price exposure and the route to repayment are important parts of the assessment.

The financing requirement should remain connected to the commercial transaction and its downside risks.

03

Asset funding

Asset requirements can relate to the equipment or infrastructure supporting a commodity business. The assessment considers the asset, its purpose, revenue or repayment logic, timing and the responsibilities of the relevant parties.

The appropriate funding route depends on the asset and the commercial case. No standard structure is assumed to fit every requirement.

04

Offtake arrangements

An offtake can connect future production or product availability with a buyer’s requirements. It may also influence the funding case by providing a defined commercial route for the material.

Specification, pricing, delivery, volume flexibility, performance conditions and contractual duration need to work together. An offtake proposal is not itself a funding commitment or a guarantee of purchase.

05

A credible capital brief

Start with the commodity or asset, the amount and purpose of funding, the transaction timeline, the repayment source and the supporting commercial arrangements. Further information and documentation can then be scoped for a more detailed assessment.

Funding and offtake outcomes remain subject to assessment, counterparty agreement, due diligence and definitive documentation.
