Make an enquiry
01

Tantalite and niobium-bearing materials

Enquiries should identify the material, contained elements, supporting analyses and origin documentation. Composition and the intended commercial route determine the assessment; a general product label is not enough.

02

Antimony and tungsten

Specify the product form, grade and relevant impurities. Quantity, location, supporting documentation and the requirements of the proposed recipient complete the brief.

03

Lithium

The chemistry and product form must be stated clearly. Mineral and chemical products should not be treated as interchangeable; specification and the intended recipient determine the commercial requirement.

04

Ilmenite and zircon

Provide mineral composition, product form, sizing and supporting quality data. The intended application and recipient’s specification determine the commercial requirement.

05

Documentation matters

Origin, traceability, product classification and applicable handling requirements should be addressed for the material concerned. Any necessary testing, approvals or permissions are specific to the proposed transaction.
