Make an enquiry
01

Cement

Enquiries should identify the required cement type and applicable standard, packing or bulk basis, destination and delivery schedule. Product documentation and the recipient’s requirements form part of the assessment.

02

Clinker

Clinker enquiries should set out the required quality information, indicative quantity and intended receiving arrangements. Supporting analyses, handling requirements and the delivery programme define the commercial brief.

03

Pozzolana

Pozzolana requirements should identify the material type, relevant composition or performance requirements and sizing. Suitability for the intended use must be established rather than inferred from the product name.

04

Clear delivery requirements

Bulk handling, packing, loading, discharge and storage responsibilities should be defined for the proposed transaction. The catalogue does not imply ownership of production or logistics assets.
