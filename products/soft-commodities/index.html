A specific requirement?

Start with the product, objective and timing.

Make an enquiry
01

Soya

Soya enquiries should identify the required product form, intended use and quality parameters. Moisture, relevant composition, quantity, packing and delivery requirements help define the commercial brief.

02

Maize

Specify the required maize type and grade, intended application, moisture and relevant quality or testing requirements. Packing or bulk basis, quantity and delivery timing should be agreed for the receiving market.

03

Sugar beans

Sugar bean enquiries should state the variety, grade, moisture, acceptable defect levels and packing format. Quality documentation and the delivery programme should reflect the buyer’s requirements.

04

Popcorn

Popcorn requirements should identify the kernel type and quality, intended use and any relevant performance or testing requirements. Moisture, packing, quantity and delivery timing form part of the assessment.

05

Coffee

Coffee enquiries should specify the product form, quality or grade, origin preference where relevant and packing requirements. Supporting quality information, quantity and timing provide a clear basis for the commercial discussion.

06

Sugar

Specify refined or brown sugar, the required grade, quality parameters and packing. The proposed quantity, delivery basis and timing are assessed alongside documentation and destination requirements.

07

Wheat

Wheat requirements should identify the grade, intended milling or feed application and relevant quality parameters. Moisture, protein or other testing requirements, quantity and delivery basis should be set out early.

08

Citrus fruit

Citrus enquiries should identify the fruit type or variety, grade, sizing, maturity requirements and packing. Delivery windows, handling needs and applicable market documentation are considered for the specific requirement.

09

Nuts

Nut enquiries should identify the type, shelled or unshelled form, grade and quality requirements. Moisture, packing and handling conditions should be aligned with the intended use and delivery programme.

10

Commercial planning

Quality documentation, inspection, packing, handling and payment conditions should be established early. Fresh-produce requirements also need clearly defined delivery and receiving arrangements. No origin, fixed pack size, inventory or availability commitment is implied by the catalogue.
