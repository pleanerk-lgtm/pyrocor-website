01

Thermal coal

Thermal coal requirements are considered against the intended industrial heat or power application. Calorific value, ash, moisture, sulphur and sizing help define the specification alongside quantity and delivery timing.

The agreed quality basis and supporting analysis should be clear before pricing and delivery terms are assessed.

02

Coal peas

Coal peas are considered as a screened coal size fraction, with the required size range agreed for the receiving equipment and application. An enquiry should identify the acceptable size distribution, quality requirements and delivery programme.

No universal size or calorific grade is assumed from the label alone; product parameters are defined for each requirement.

03

Other sized coal: nuts and lump

Sized coal requirements can include nuts and lump fractions. The required particle-size range, quality, handling conditions and intended application should be specified at the outset.

Screening tolerances, moisture and consistency form part of the commercial brief. Availability and suitability are assessed against the agreed specification.

04

Coal fines and duff

Fines and duff requirements are assessed around particle-size distribution, moisture, calorific value and the receiving process. Handling characteristics and suitability for the intended application need to be considered together.

The product description should be supported by relevant quality information rather than treated as a standardised grade.

05

Export-grade coal

Export-grade coal is assessed against the buyer’s agreed quality and delivery specification. It is not a single universal grade: calorific value, ash, moisture, sulphur, sizing and the applicable testing basis must be defined.

Enquiries should identify the destination or delivery basis, shipment quantity, loading window, inspection requirements and pricing reference. No fixed origin, export programme or supply commitment is implied by the catalogue.

06

Quality and delivery requirements

Provide the required coal form, intended use, available analysis or target quality, size requirements, indicative quantity and delivery schedule. Specify the calorific-value and testing basis to avoid comparing unlike specifications.

Pricing, inspection, payment and transport responsibilities should be aligned with the agreed product and commercial terms.

07

Advisory and capital alongside the trade

Coal pricing references, currency exposure and the timing of funding can influence the transaction. Advisory and Capital can be considered alongside the physical coal requirement where relevant to the agreed scope.
