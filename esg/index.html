01

Our principles

Responsible trade begins with the questions asked before a transaction is considered. Our principles guide a thoughtful, risk-based approach to sourcing and engagement. They set expectations for how we consider opportunities, alongside product-specific commercial assessment.

02

Responsible sourcing and provenance

When considering a material, we seek to understand the information available about its origin, producer, material classification, ownership and provenance. The level and type of information needed can vary with the product, route, counterparties and circumstances. Documentation is considered in context, with the purpose of identifying questions that need to be addressed before an opportunity progresses.

03

Environmental considerations

We consider environmental and handling implications appropriate to the product and proposed route, including the nature of the material, transport, storage, processing and any applicable requirements. This is a practical consideration in the commercial review; the relevant obligations and responsibilities remain specific to the material and transaction.

04

People and working conditions

We expect suppliers and counterparties to respect human rights, avoid child or forced labour, and promote safe working conditions. Those expectations inform engagement and conversation, while the information available and responsibilities of the parties are assessed in the context of the opportunity.

05

Governance and commercial integrity

Commercial integrity calls for clarity about roles, responsibilities and the information that supports a transaction. We expect dealings to be conducted transparently and without bribery or improper influence, and we consider ownership, documentation and commercial rationale as part of the wider picture.

06

Risk-based engagement

Responsible sourcing is not a uniform checklist. We apply judgment according to the product, origin, counterparty, transaction structure and information available. Where concerns arise, we may seek additional information, adjust the scope of review or determine that further engagement is not appropriate. Any decision remains conditional on the facts and commercial assessment of the specific opportunity.

07

Raise a concern

If you have a concern connected to a PYROCOR commercial opportunity, email admin@pyrocor.co.za with a brief, non-sensitive account of the relevant facts. Please do not send confidential contracts, personal data or sensitive commercial information in an initial message. We will consider the information in the context of the matter raised.
