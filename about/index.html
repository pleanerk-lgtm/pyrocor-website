01

What “Beyond Trading” means

A physical commodity transaction is rarely defined by a headline price alone. The material’s characteristics, the pricing reference, the delivery programme, the payment terms and the capital requirement can each change its commercial outcome.

Our commercial perspective is informed by 20 years of industry experience.

Beyond Trading means considering those factors together. It also means being clear about the role PYROCOR plays and the responsibilities of each party.

02

Our market focus

Coal and complex minerals are the lead markets. Alongside them, our product coverage includes manganese and chrome ores, minor and battery materials, selected agricultural commodities and cementitious materials.

The Products section identifies the material families considered. Individual requirements are assessed against the relevant specification, timing and commercial context.

03

A bespoke approach

We begin with the requirement rather than a standard solution. A seller may need a route to an appropriate buyer. A buyer may need a clearly specified material and delivery programme. A stakeholder may need to understand market exposure or establish how a trade could be financed.

The first step is to establish the objective, the information available and the work required. Trading, Advisory and Capital can then be considered in the context of that requirement. The scope of each engagement is agreed separately.

04

How we work

Understand the opportunity. Establish the material, objectives, timing and commercial constraints.

Define the scope. Identify the role required of PYROCOR and the information needed to assess it.

Develop the approach. Bring product, market, risk and capital considerations together where relevant.

Agree the next steps. Set out responsibilities and the conditions required before commitments are made.

05

Commercial discretion

The website explains our areas of focus. Counterparty information, transaction terms and detailed structures are handled through direct engagement rather than published as part of a public catalogue.
