People & principles

Working principles

Commodity trading brings together product knowledge, market understanding and commercial decisions. Our approach keeps those questions connected across Trading, Advisory and Capital.

01

Understand before proposing

Establish the material, the decision and the information available before developing an approach.

02

Be clear about responsibility

Define who is doing what and which conditions must be met before commitments are made.

03

Bring the workstreams together

Consider the physical trade, price and currency exposure, and capital requirements where relevant.

04

Respect commercial information

Keep confidential transaction details out of the public catalogue and agree appropriate information-sharing arrangements.

Our responsibilities

Sustainability and responsible sourcing

Responsible trade depends on how people work as well as what is traded. Respect for human rights, safe working conditions and commercial integrity inform the relationships we seek to build.

We consider provenance, environmental and handling implications, and the responsibilities of each party in the context of the material and transaction. Our ESG approach explains the questions that guide this engagement.
